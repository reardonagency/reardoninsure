License, Permit & Right-of-Way Bonds in Colorado

Need a bond to get your license, pull a permit or work in the public right-of-way? Many are issued instantly with approved credit.

Bonds written through CNA Surety

License and permit bonds are the most common type of commercial surety bond. A city, county, state agency or licensing board requires one as a condition of giving you a license, a permit or access to public property. The bond guarantees that you will follow the rules attached to it, and it gives the public a way to be made whole if you do not.

Reardon Agency places license and permit bonds with CNA Surety, whose bond lines include license and permit, public official, miscellaneous and federal bonds. For highway permit and right-of-way bonds, CNA now instant-issues bonds up to $250,000 with approved credit.

Highway Permit & Right-of-Way Bonds

Companies that dig, drill, haul or build near public roads usually need a permit from a state or local transportation authority, and that permit often comes with a bond. The bond guarantees that you will repair damage to the roadway or right-of-way and comply with the permit conditions.

CNA Surety has streamlined the approval for these bonds:

  • Instant issue up to $250,000 with approved credit
  • No signatures needed on bonds under $100,000
  • No credit check on bonds under $25,000

That means a contractor who needs to start work can often have the bond in hand the same day.

Typical users

  • Excavation and utility contractors
  • Paving, concrete and landscaping companies
  • Telecom and fiber installers
  • Trucking and oversize hauling operations
  • Developers working with public improvements

The requirement comes from the agency, not from us

Each city, county, district and state agency sets its own bond amount, wording and filing process, and those details change. Colorado cities and counties handle many licenses and permits locally, so the answer for Colorado Springs can differ from Pueblo, Denver or El Paso County. Bring us the agency’s bond form or written requirement and we will match it exactly.

Cost

License and permit bonds are priced as a small percentage of the bond amount, and many carry a minimum premium. Strong credit generally means a lower rate, and the bonds with the lowest requirements, like those under $25,000 with no credit check, are quick and simple. Request a quote with the bond form and Ryan will give you the exact premium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

What is a license and permit bond?

A license and permit bond is a surety bond that a government agency requires before it issues a license or permit. It guarantees the license holder will follow the laws and rules that apply and gives the public a way to recover losses if they do not.

What is a highway permit bond or right-of-way bond?

A highway permit or right-of-way bond is required by a transportation authority or local government when you work in or access public roads and right-of-way. It guarantees you will comply with the permit and repair any damage. CNA Surety offers instant issue up to $250,000 with approved credit.

How much does a license or permit bond cost?

The premium is a percentage of the bond amount, with the exact rate depending on your credit and the type of bond. Many small bonds are quite affordable. Send the requirement to Ryan for an exact price.

Can I get a permit bond without a credit check?

For highway permit and right-of-way bonds under $25,000, CNA Surety does not require a credit check. Larger bonds use approved credit, and bonds under $100,000 do not need signatures.

How fast can I get a bond for my permit?

Highway permit and right-of-way bonds up to $250,000 can be issued instantly with approved credit. Other bonds are usually underwritten quickly once the requirement and the applicant information are in hand.

Which license bonds does Colorado require?

Colorado licensing is a mix of state, county and city requirements, and it varies by trade and location. Check with the licensing authority, or send the requirement to Ryan and he will confirm the bond and amount.

What is a public official bond?

A public official bond guarantees that an elected or appointed official will faithfully perform their duties and account for public funds. A statute, ordinance or governing board may require it. CNA Surety includes public official bonds among its commercial bond lines.
