Myth vs. Fact

Myth

Life insurance costs more than most people can afford.

Fact

A policy can cost about as much as a monthly streaming subscription — often far less than people expect.

Myth

The life insurance I have through work is enough.

Fact

Workplace coverage is often limited, tied to your job, and may not match what your family would actually need.

Myth

Healthy people don't need life insurance.

Fact

Premiums are generally lowest when you're healthy — locking in a policy now protects your family and your rate.

Myth

I'm single with no dependents, so it wouldn't help me.

Fact

Coverage can still cover personal debt, co-signed loans, or funeral costs so they don't fall on your family.

Myth

Life insurance only matters if the business owner passes away.

Fact

It can also be a useful piece of business planning, including managing your company's tax picture — Ryan can walk through the details with you (not tax advice — always confirm specifics with your tax professional).

Source: 2025 Insurance Barometer Study, Life Happens and LIMRA.
