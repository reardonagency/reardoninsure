Business Insurance, Built to Actually Fit

Most agents can offer you one carrier's rulebook. Reardon Agency works with Farmers plus a dozen specialty and wholesale markets — so your coverage fits your business, not the other way around.

Business insurance is where a lot of agents fall short. If your business doesn't fit neatly into a single carrier's checkbox — a contractor with a tricky claims history, a growing company that's outgrown its old policy, a business in an industry most carriers avoid — a one-carrier agent can only tell you no, or quote you a rate that doesn't reflect your actual risk.

This is genuinely where we specialize. Alongside Farmers, Reardon Agency has direct access to national specialty and wholesale markets that most local agencies simply don't carry. That means more ways to say yes, more competitive pricing, and coverage built around how your business actually operates.
