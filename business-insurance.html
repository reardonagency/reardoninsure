Myth vs. Fact

Myth

We're too small for hackers to bother with.

Fact

Small businesses are often easier targets than large companies, because they rarely have a dedicated security team or an incident response plan.

Myth

Our business policy already covers a data breach.

Fact

A standard BOP has no cyber coverage built in. Breach response, ransomware, and regulatory costs generally need a separate cyber liability policy.

Myth

If money gets stolen, our bank will make us whole.

Fact

Losses from scams like a spoofed invoice or a fake wire request can be hard to recover, and the cleanup costs land on the business.

Myth

Cyber coverage is too expensive for a small business.

Fact

Cyber liability coverage has become more affordable for small businesses in recent years, and a quote is the only way to know what yours would cost.

Myth

With antivirus software and good passwords, we don't need insurance.

Fact

Good security habits lower your risk but don't eliminate it. Insurance is what pays for notifying customers, restoring systems, and legal and regulatory expenses when something still gets through.

Sources: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) 2023 Internet Crime Report; Federal Trade Commission Consumer Sentinel Network Data Book 2023.
