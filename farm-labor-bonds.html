H-2A Farm Labor Contractor Bonds

Filing an H-2A application as a farm labor contractor? Get the required surety bond fast, instant issue up to $150,000 with approved credit.

Bonds written through CNA Surety

The H-2A program lets agricultural employers bring in temporary foreign workers when they cannot find enough U.S. workers. When a farm labor contractor files an H-2A application with the U.S. Department of Labor, the Department requires a surety bond. It is there to make sure the workers get paid the wages and benefits they are owed, even if the contractor cannot pay.

Reardon Agency places farm labor bonds through CNA Surety. CNA has revised its underwriting to instant-issue qualifying requests up to $150,000 with approved credit, which matters when the filing window is tight and crops will not wait.

Who Needs an H-2A Farm Labor Bond?

The bond is required of H-2A labor contractors: businesses that recruit, hire, house or transport workers and supply them to farms and ranches. The original bond is submitted with the application for temporary labor certification (Form ETA-9142A) and its appendix.

An agricultural employer filing for its own workers has different filing rules than a contractor. If you are not sure which category you fall into, check with your immigration attorney or with the Department of Labor, then bring Ryan the instructions you were given.

The bond amount is set by federal rules and generally depends on the number of workers, so we need the details of the application before quoting.

What the bond helps guarantee

  • Payment of wages owed to workers
  • Compliance with the terms of the H-2A job order
  • Other obligations the program places on the contractor

How to Get Your Bond

  1. Confirm the amount

    Use the Department of Labor’s instructions or your attorney’s advice for the required bond amount.

  2. Send us the details

    Business name, owners, the bond amount and the filing deadline. A credit review is part of approval.

  3. Instant issue

    Qualifying requests up to $150,000 with approved credit are issued right away. Larger amounts are underwritten individually.

  4. File with your application

    You receive the bond to include with your H-2A filing. Allow time for the original paperwork.

Rules and amounts are set by the federal government

The H-2A bond requirements, including amounts and wording, come from the U.S. Department of Labor and can change from year to year. This page is general information, not immigration or legal advice. Confirm your filing requirements with the Department of Labor or your attorney, and we will match the bond to them.

Cost

The premium is a percentage of the bond amount, and your rate depends on credit and business history. Because the bond is a guarantee that must be repaid if a claim is paid, approval looks at the owners’ credit. Request a quote with the bond amount and you will get an exact premium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

What is an H-2A farm labor contractor bond?

It is a surety bond that an H-2A labor contractor must file with the U.S. Department of Labor with its application. It helps guarantee that the workers are paid the wages and benefits they are owed under the H-2A program.

How much does an H-2A farm labor bond cost?

The premium is a percentage of the bond amount and depends on the owners’ credit and business history. The required amount is set by federal rules and generally depends on the crew size. Send Ryan the amount and he will quote the exact premium.

How fast can I get an H-2A bond?

CNA Surety instant-issues qualifying farm labor bonds up to $150,000 with approved credit, so many requests are completed the same day. Bonds above that amount are underwritten individually.

Do I need an H-2A bond if I am a farmer using the program?

The bond requirement applies to H-2A labor contractors. A farmer hiring workers directly follows different filing rules. Confirm which applies to you with the Department of Labor or your attorney, then bring us the requirement.

Does Colorado have its own farm labor contractor bond?

The H-2A bond is a federal requirement that applies in every state. State or local rules vary. Check with the Colorado Department of Labor and Employment, and send Ryan anything you are told you need.

What information do I need to apply?

Your business name and ownership, the bond amount from your filing instructions, your contact information and your filing deadline. CNA Surety reviews the owners’ credit as part of approval.
