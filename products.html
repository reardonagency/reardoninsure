Umbrella Policy

Extra protection above your limits

An umbrella policy provides an extra layer of liability coverage that kicks in when your auto or home policy limits are exhausted. It's one of the best values in insurance — and most people skip it.

  • $1M–$5M+ in additional liability coverage
  • Covers auto and home liability above policy limits
  • Protects assets in lawsuits
  • Covers incidents your other policies may miss
  • Affordable — often $150–$300/year
  • Ideal for homeowners, business owners, and parents

Sola Wind & Hail Coverage

A smarter way to handle your home policy's wind/hail deductible

Colorado hail and wind claims come with steep deductibles — and older roofs get depreciated payouts on top of that. Sola is a standalone policy that sits alongside your home insurance and helps cover that gap. It has no deductible of its own, uses National Weather Service storm data to pay claims (no adjuster needed), and doesn't touch your home insurance premium or claims record. It's not a replacement for your homeowners policy — it's protection for the out-of-pocket costs your homeowners policy leaves behind.

💵

$0 Deductible

Sola's own policy has no deductible — it's built to offset your home policy's wind/hail deductible.

⚡

Fast, Simple Payouts

Storm data confirms your claim automatically — no adjuster visits, no waiting weeks for an inspection.

🛡️

Doesn't Affect Your Home Policy

A Sola claim isn't recorded on your CLUE report and won't raise your home insurance premium.

🏦

Reinsured & Secure

Backed by an A-rated reinsurer out of Lloyd's of London.

01
Check for Damage

After a wind, hail, or tornado event, look for visible damage — leaks, broken windows, or roof damage.

02

Sola confirms the storm using National Weather Service data — no adjuster needed.
