🧱 A Note on SCIP Construction Panels

Coons Construction (our General Contractor referral above) builds with SCIP panels — Structural Concrete Insulated Panels — a wall system with notably strong fire, wind, and seismic performance compared to traditional wood-frame construction. If you're building new or considering a rebuild, it's worth knowing this option exists and understanding how it performs.
