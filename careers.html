Business Insurance Associate

📍 Colorado Springs, CO  |  Full-Time  |  On-Site  |  Base + Commission

Supports the agency's commercial lines growth by helping local business owners understand their coverage needs and connecting them with tailored insurance solutions. This role blends sales, client relationship management, and underwriting support — prospecting, quoting, and onboarding new commercial clients while assisting with renewals, policy changes, and service requests.

Ideal for someone who enjoys working with small business owners, has strong communication skills, and is motivated by building long-term professional relationships.

Sales & Business Development

Prospect local business owners through outreach, referrals, and community involvement
Identify coverage needs and present commercial solutions
Quote BOP, commercial auto, workers' comp, general liability, and more
Manage pipeline activity and onboard new commercial clients

Client Service & Support

Assist with policy changes, billing, certificates of insurance, and claims coordination
Maintain accurate client records and documentation
Support renewal reviews and identify cross-sell opportunities
Provide responsive service that strengthens client retention

Operational & Administrative

Work with the agency owner to prioritize leads and manage workflows
Ensure applications and underwriting documents are completed accurately
Maintain compliance with Farmers, state, and agency standards

Required: Strong communication skills; a sales-driven mindset comfortable with outreach and prospecting; solid organization and attention to detail; ability to work independently and with the team; proficient with office software and willing to learn agency management systems; high school diploma or equivalent; ability to pass a standard background check.

Licensing: Active Colorado Property & Casualty license required (or willingness to obtain — agency support available). Life & Health license preferred but not required.

Preferred: Experience in commercial insurance, small business sales, banking, lending, or other B2B environments; comfort discussing operational risks with business owners; familiarity with Farmers commercial products (BOP, GL, WC, CA, etc.) is a plus.

How We Hire

Built Around Fit, Not Just a Resume

A resume tells you what someone has done. It doesn't tell you whether they'll thrive here. That's why we use IdealTraits, a science-based assessment built specifically for insurance agencies, as part of our hiring process.

This isn't about disqualifying people or checking a box. It's a two-way tool: it helps us build a team with the right mix of skills and personalities to support each other, and it helps you get an honest, research-backed read on where your own strengths are best put to use — whether that's here, in a different role than you expected, or somewhere else entirely. The goal is a team and a career path that actually fits, for both sides.
